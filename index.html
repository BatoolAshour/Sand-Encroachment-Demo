    How to read this dashboard

    The road simulation reproduces the sand-and-road box experiment with fixed scenarios or live Arduino readings. Satellite and weather views describe the real research routes (Al-Ahsa – Salwa, Abqaiq – Salasil, Al-Ahsa – Riyadh). They are shown beside the experiment, never mixed into its readings, risk label or log.

      Rule-based detection (colour segmentation, thresholds, texture, morphology) on real Sentinel-2 pixels, not validated in the field. Route centrelines and 2 km study areas come unchanged from the research KMZ files. Percentages are shares of clear pixels in the segment's part of the study area; distance is from the road centreline to the nearest detected sand pixel in it. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data.

      Detection, not prediction

      What this shows: where sand is visible in one Sentinel-2 image on the chosen date, measured by fixed, explainable rules.

      What it does not do: forecast where sand will move or how risk will change. Prediction is a separate, future phase and is not part of this dashboard.
